# Doraemon and the Super Saiyan: Battle of Space and Time

On an ordinary afternoon, Nobita was sitting at his desk staring blankly as usual. His homework was piled as high as a mountain, and he hadn't even started the first page. Doraemon was next to him, flipping through a comic book and sighing from time to time, feeling that this kid was as unreliable as ever. Just as their lives were carrying on normally, a bright light suddenly descended from the sky, shaking the entire room. Out of the light stepped a blond-haired boy wearing battle armor and exuding an astonishing aura; he was the Super Saiyan from the future—Trunks. As soon as he appeared, he spoke astonishing words: the Earth of the future was about to be destroyed by dark forces, and he had come to seek Doraemon's help.

Doraemon and Nobita were shocked upon hearing this, but they also read an undeniable resolve in Trunks' determined eyes. Trunks explained that the enemy in the future was no ordinary villain, but an entity known as the "Dark Saiyan." Created by an evil scientist who cloned and modified Vegeta's genes, his power was beyond imagination. This enemy not only possessed Saiyan combat strength but could also manipulate distorted time energy, making him virtually invincible. Trunks had been fighting alone for years, but every encounter ended in a crushing defeat. He said, "Technology is the only weapon missing from my era, and you happen to possess it."

And so, Doraemon, taking Trunks and Nobita along, activated the Time Machine and traveled to that collapsing future world. The sight before them was shocking: cities were reduced to ruins, the ground was crisscrossed with fissures, and an oppressive black mist drifted in the sky. Trunks said this was exactly what the Dark Saiyan had wrought—almost all life had been wiped out, leaving only him to struggle on. Although Nobita felt afraid, seeing innocent humans suffer gradually ignited his fighting spirit. Doraemon calmly analyzed the situation and decided to use his three strongest secret gadgets to combat the dark forces.

The three secret gadgets were: The "Copy Cape," which could temporarily grant super combat power. The "Time-Stopping Watch," which could pause time for five seconds. The "Portable Hyperbolic Time Chamber," which allowed one to complete a year of training in just one minute. Nobita was pushed into the chamber, undergoing intensive training inside. Although only a few minutes passed in real time, he experienced a full year of rigorous ascetic training. At first, he was still weak, wanting to give up and run away, but when he thought of Shizuka, his parents, and Doraemon's determined gaze, he finally gritted his teeth and persevered. After coming out, his body and mind were completely renewed, and his eyes held a new level of maturity and confidence.

The final battle broke out in front of the Dark Saiyan's aerial fortress. Trunks attacked first, unleashing his full power in a head-on confrontation with the enemy. Doraemon supported him using the Anywhere Door and gadgets, creating chaos from all directions to suppress the enemy's space-time abilities as much as possible. However, the Dark Saiyan was too powerful; Trunks alone couldn't even suppress him, let alone defeat him. Just as Trunks was about to be struck down, Nobita, wearing the Copy Cape, broke through his fear and leaped down from high above. His fists burned with a golden aura of light, aiming straight for the enemy's heart.

The Time-Stopping Watch activated at the crucial moment, bringing the world to a standstill. Nobita used these short five seconds to approach the enemy's blind spot. Concentrating all his strength, a heavy punch pierced through the Dark Saiyan's energy core, triggering a massive energy backlash. The Dark Saiyan screamed as he disintegrated into shattered light, the black mist in the sky instantly dissipated, and sunlight once again sprinkled across the earth. Trunks fell to the ground, looking at the once-cowardly boy before him, and revealed a gratified smile. He knew that this time, it was Nobita who had saved the world.

After the battle, the future world began to recover; plants regrew, and humanity rebuilt their homes. As he said goodbye, Trunks tightly grasped Nobita's hand and said, "You are the most special warrior I have ever met." Doraemon was also proud of Nobita, saying he had finally truly grown up. The three stood on a hill, looking at the newly brightened horizon in the distance, feeling a peace they had never known. Subsequently, Doraemon and Nobita took the Time Machine back to their own era, and everything seemed to return to tranquility.

After returning to the present, Nobita seemed like a changed person; he no longer complained easily or shirked his responsibilities. He earnestly finished his homework, helped his mother with grocery shopping, and even voluntarily practiced sports, leaving Doraemon speechless with surprise. He knew this wasn't a passing whim, but the result of Nobita's true inner growth. Nobita would sometimes gaze up at the sky, lost in thought, as if he could still see those ruins and the hope of rebirth in the future world. He wouldn't say it aloud, but he would forever remember that battle in his heart.

A few days later, a scene suddenly appeared on the television news: a blond-haired boy repelling a runaway robot on the streets, sparking crowds of onlookers and speculation among citizens. Nobita put down the textbook in his hands and looked at Doraemon, and the two smiled in tacit understanding. Perhaps Trunks had returned, or perhaps a new enemy was approaching. The adventure was never truly over, and they were already well-prepared. No matter how turbulent space and time might become, they would forever fight side by side.
